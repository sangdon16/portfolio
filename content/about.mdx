I’m a UX designer at DigiCert, working across marketing web experiences and reusable components.

My work often starts with a question that has several reasonable answers: how to organize a complex navigation, keep a form usable as requirements grow, or make a component work across different content and screen sizes.

I explore alternatives, examine edge cases, and collaborate with content and development colleagues to shape a direction I can explain.

Visual craft is a strength I bring to the work. I also care about the reasoning behind the interface: what we considered, what we prioritized, and what we chose to leave behind.

Outside my DigiCert work, I explore independent products, including UgnAI Response, and collaborative challenges such as SplitSafe.
